Research

My interests run from the fundamental theory of electromagnetic scattering to antenna hardware that gets measured in the lab and used in networks.

Research themes

1

Antennas & RIS for 6G

At Intracom Telecom I lead the design, prototyping and measurement of phased-array, reflectarray and microstrip antenna systems at 24–30 GHz and E-band (71–76 and 81–86 GHz). I also work on reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and terahertz metasurfaces in Horizon Europe SNS-JU projects (TERRAMETA, TERA6G, SPRINTER). I co-advise a doctoral candidate (University of Patras) on metamaterial-enhanced beam-steering microstrip arrays.

2

Wave-based analog computing

At Penn with N. Engheta I introduced metadevices that solve equations and invert matrices using electromagnetic waves, including a custom-built reconfigurable RF proof-of-concept device. The work has appeared in Light: Science & Applications and ACS Photonics, and in Nature Communications (2025), where a metastructure designs other metastructures. It was featured in Physics (APS) and as a Nature Reviews Electrical Engineering Research Highlight. Open code and raw data are on figshare.

3

Scattering, Mie resonances & characteristic modes

My doctoral work (advisor A. Sihvola, Aalto) gives a unified picture of resonances in spheres, polyhedra and superquadric particles. The invited review Light Scattering by a Dielectric Sphere: Perspectives on the Mie Resonances is among my most cited papers. With P. Ylä-Oijala I helped extend the theory of characteristic modes to penetrable bodies.

4

Biomedical & transparent-conductor antennas

With P. Kosmas and colleagues, and with Meta Materials, I developed metamaterial-inspired wearable pads for coupling EM energy to biological tissue (IEEE TAP 2025), and antennas on optically transparent conductive films (Frontiers in Antennas and Propagation 2026).

5

Underwater metasurface communications

As a part-time consulting scientist for the National Hellenic Research Foundation (group of O. Tsilipakos), I contribute to metasurface-based underwater communication in the European Defence Fund project AVALON.

6

Open research code

I try to release code and data with my papers: the wavecheck Python tool and the figshare data set of the analog-computing hardware (firmware, drivers, instrument control, simulation).
