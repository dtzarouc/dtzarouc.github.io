Research

My interests run from the fundamental theory of electromagnetic scattering to antenna hardware that gets measured in the lab and used in networks.

Research themes

1

Antennas & RIS for 6G

At Intracom Telecom I lead the design, prototyping and measurement of phased-array, reflectarray and microstrip antenna systems at 24–30 GHz and E-band (71–76 and 81–86 GHz). I also work on reconfigurable intelligent surfaces and terahertz metasurfaces in Horizon Europe SNS-JU projects. I co-advise a doctoral candidate (University of Patras) on metamaterial-enhanced beam-steering microstrip arrays.

Read / explore: iWAT 2026 patch-array paper · THz research at Intracom (ISVLSI 2025) · TERRAMETA · TERA6G · SPRINTER · Intelligent metasurfaces review (Adv. Opt. Mater. 2020)

2

Wave-based analog computing

At Penn with N. Engheta I introduced metadevices that solve equations and invert matrices using electromagnetic waves, including a custom-built reconfigurable RF proof-of-concept device. The work has appeared in Light: Science & Applications, ACS Photonics and Nature Communications (2025), where a metastructure designs other metastructures. Open code and raw data are public.

Read / explore: Nature Communications 2025 · Light: Sci. & Appl. 2022 · ACS Photonics 2022 · Hardware & data (figshare) · APS Physics news: analog optical computing · Diffractive optical computing review

3

Scattering, Mie resonances & characteristic modes

My doctoral work (advisor A. Sihvola, Aalto) gives a unified picture of resonances in spheres, polyhedra and superquadric particles. The invited review on Mie resonances is among my most cited papers. With P. Ylä-Oijala I helped extend the theory of characteristic modes to penetrable bodies.

Read / explore: Mie resonances review (Appl. Sci. 2018) · Resonant scattering of a dielectric sphere (TAP 2017)

4

Biomedical & transparent-conductor antennas

With P. Kosmas and colleagues, and with Meta Materials, I developed metamaterial-inspired wearable pads for coupling EM energy to biological tissue, and antennas on optically transparent conductive films.

Read / explore: Wearable pad (IEEE TAP 2025) · Transparent-film antenna (Front. Antennas Propag. 2026) · Metamaterials for biomedical applications (EPJ AM 2024)

5

Underwater metasurface communications

As a part-time consulting scientist for the National Hellenic Research Foundation (group of O. Tsilipakos), I contribute to metasurface-based underwater communication in the European Defence Fund project AVALON.

Read / explore: AVALON (partner page, UPC) · Neuromorphic nanophotonics review (arXiv)

6

Open research code

I try to release code and data with my papers: the wavecheck Python tool and the figshare data set of the analog-computing hardware (firmware, drivers, instrument control, simulation).

Read / explore: wavecheck on GitHub · Analog-computing hardware & data · All repositories
